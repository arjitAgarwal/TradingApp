NOTE: ONE THING I WON'T CHANGE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Page 4 asks to remove the DEMO banner and the "Paper Trade" title,
and other pages ask to make everything identical to Groww.

Together, and used "live", that would make the app look like a real
Groww account showing real trades and real profits. That can mislead
people watching, and in India it can run into SEBI rules for
finfluencers.

What I WILL do:
  - Make the layout, colours, fonts and flow feel like Groww.
  - Remove the DEMO / time tags under prices and the wallet icon.

What I will KEEP:
  - The DEMO banner, ONLY when the app is in Demo mode (fake prices).
  - A small "Paper" tag in the header in FYERS (real prices) mode.

If his live sessions are for teaching or practice, the "Paper" tag
also protects him.
